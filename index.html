Core proposed solution
  • 1. Speech adaptation: designed for broken, interrupted, hesitant, or atypical speech.
  • 2. Current input: shows exactly what PaceVoice thinks the user said.
  • 3. Conversation history: keeps previous conversations available to read and review.
  • 4. User-controlled pace: gives the user more time instead of forcing a fast response.
  • 5. Multimodal support: combines voice, text, repetition, and confirmation.
